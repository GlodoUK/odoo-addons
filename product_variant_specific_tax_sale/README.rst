Product Variant Specific Tax Sale
=================================

Allows taxes to be defined on product variants.

In some industries, product variants may require different tax treatments than their parent product template.

For example, in the UK clothing sector, children's clothing is zero-rated for VAT, while adult clothing is taxed at the standard 20% rate.

A single product template such as "T-Shirt" may have variants based on size, where sizes intended for children must be treated as VAT-exempt, while adult sizes remain taxable.
